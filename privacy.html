Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-06

What we collect

  • Uploaded model files, the optional request text and the scale you enter, and the files we produce from them.
  • Your IP address, kept in server memory only to enforce the upload limits (per hour and per day).
  • We do not ask for names, e-mail addresses or accounts.

How long

Uploaded files, results and job status are deleted automatically 24 hours after upload. Limit counters reset within a day.

Where it is processed

  • Files are processed on our own servers in the Republic of Korea.
  • If you write a request, the agent mode may send the request text and the repair reports (numbers such as edge counts, not the geometry) to Anthropic's Claude API to choose the repair steps.

Third parties on this page

  • GitHub Pages hosts this page and may log visits (GitHub's privacy statement applies).
  • The 3D viewer library is loaded from jsDelivr.
  • Advertising: this site may show ads by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com; see how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Your choices and contact

Do not upload confidential models or models you have no right to share. To ask for earlier deletion of a job, or for any question, open an issue at github.com/JaekunE2/JaekunE2.github.io/issues with the job id (do not post the file).

보관 기간

올린 파일, 결과 파일, 작업 상태는 업로드 24시간 뒤 자동 삭제됩니다. 한도 집계는 하루 안에 초기화됩니다.

처리 장소

  • 파일은 대한민국에 있는 운영자 서버에서 처리합니다.
