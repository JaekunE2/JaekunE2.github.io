How it works

    

      
1. DiagnoseOpen and non-manifold edges, loose pieces, free-standing parts thinner than the nozzle.

      
2. SolidifyVoxel fill under a memory guard, marching cubes, topology-preserving decimation.

      
3. Split and restoreExact booleans for inserts with clearance and added pieces.

      
4. ColourSource materials to N filament parts in one 3MF.

      
5. Prove itSlice before and after with CuraEngine and compare the toolpaths.

    

    
An agent (Claude) reads the diagnosis and the request ("keep the lettering, make the stair removable")
      and chooses the steps; without one, a fixed pipeline runs the same tools.
