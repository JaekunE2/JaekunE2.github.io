      What breaks a print, and what we do about it
      
    
      Each of these makes a slicer drop walls, fill the wrong side or skip layers.
      
    
      01
      Open edges (holes)
      What
      An edge used by only one face: the surface is not closed.
      In the slicer
      The slicer cannot tell inside from outside, so walls and floors go missing.
      printdoctor
      The model is rebuilt as a closed solid; the holes are filled from the surrounding surface.
      02
      Non-manifold edges
      What
      An edge shared by three or more faces, typical of unwelded CAD and architectural exports.
      In the slicer
      Cross-sections become ambiguous and layers come out partly empty.
      printdoctor
      Overlapping and touching pieces are merged into one solid with a single clean surface.
      03
      Flipped faces
      What
      Neighbouring faces wound in opposite directions.
      In the slicer
      Parts of the model are treated as inside-out.
      printdoctor
      The rebuilt surface is oriented consistently, outward.
      04
      Zero-area faces
      What
      Triangles collapsed to a line or point.
      In the slicer
      Slicer warnings, tiny gaps, failed booleans.
      printdoctor
      Removed in the rebuild.
      05
      Loose shells
      What
      Hundreds of separate pieces: window frames, panels, labels.
      In the slicer
      Floating specks print as strings or fall off; overlaps double the walls.
      printdoctor
      Pieces are joined into one body; specks smaller than a nozzle are dropped.
      06
      Parts thinner than the nozzle
      What
      Free-standing rails, rods and antennas under about 0.45 mm.
      In the slicer
      They print as broken strands or not at all.
      printdoctor
      They are detected and removed, while relief lying on a surface (lettering, patterns) is kept.

      FAQ
      
    
      How do I repair an STL online?
      Drop the file into the tool above. The free check shows what is wrong; press Repair and download the result a minute later.
      Which formats can I upload?
      STL, OBJ, PLY, GLB/GLTF, 3MF and OFF, up to 50 MB. The result is an STL.
      Does it change the shape?
      It rebuilds the surface at about 0.2 mm resolution for models up to 25 cm, so detail finer than that softens. The deviation from your original is measured and shown.
      What does the request box do?
      With a request, an agent decides the repair steps, for example removing railings but keeping lettering. Agent jobs have a daily limit; without a request the automatic pipeline runs.
      Is it free? Can I use the result commercially?
      Yes, the beta is free (supported by ads), and you keep all rights to your models and the results.
      Who can see my file?
      Only the server that processes it. Files and results are deleted automatically after 24 hours; see the privacy policy.
      My model is in centimetres or inches
      Set the scale: 10 for centimetres to millimetres, 25.4 for inches.
